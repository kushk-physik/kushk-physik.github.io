Projects

Computational and research projects, from my current work back to my student days at NISER.

Current computational projects

2026 – now
Electromagnetic fields in ICCING, with Prof. Jaki Noronha-Hostler, UIUC
Evolving the fluctuating charge distributions generated by the ICCING Monte Carlo framework in space- and time-dependent electromagnetic fields, implemented in C++ with care for charge conservation and event-by-event correlations.
2026
Monte Carlo EKRT model, LBNL & RIKEN iTHEMS, with Dr. Yuuka Kanakubo and Dr. Lipei Du
Event-by-event initial conditions for heavy-ion collisions.
2025 – 2026
Kinetic solver for pre-equilibrium gluon pressure, UFF
An effective kinetic model, solved numerically in Python and validated against established results, for magnetic-field-induced anisotropic gluon pressure in heavy-ion collisions.

Research projects

Jul 2020 – Jul 2021
Relativistic magnetohydrodynamics, master’s thesis with Prof. Amaresh Jaiswal, NISER
Derived second-order viscous, non-resistive magnetohydrodynamic equations for the dissipative quantities in the relaxation-time approximation, and explored resistive fluids in magnetic fields with the Chapman–Enskog method.
Jan – May 2020
Functional Schrödinger equation, with Dr. Yogesh Kumar Srivastava, NISER
Learned functional calculus for bosonic and fermionic variables, and solved the Schrödinger equation in the functional representation for free scalar, photon and spinor field theories.
May – Jul 2019
DEM modelling of rapid granular avalanches, with Dr. Gaurav Bhutani, IIT Mandi
Simulated granular avalanches with the discrete element method in LIGGGHTS, studying kinetic sieving of particles flowing down an inclined plane and how force and velocity affect it.
Jan – May 2019
Relativistic hydrodynamics, with Prof. Amaresh Jaiswal, NISER
Studied general relativity and the kinetic-theory foundations of fluid dynamics, from the Newtonian to the relativistic regime: equations of state and the many forms of the relativistic hydrodynamic equations.
May – Jul 2018
Optical properties of semiconductor nanostructures, with Dr. Sudakshina Prusty, NISER
Simulated spectroscopic data for indirect-gap nanostructures with a quantum-confinement model; tight-binding results for silicon showed the band-gap blue shift and growing overlap of band-edge states as the nanostructures shrink.
May – Jul 2017
Statistical error analysis with ROOT, with Dr. Prolay Mal, NISER
Probability distributions, parameter testing and statistical error analysis in ROOT, alongside a basic study of particle physics.
Aug – Sep 2017
All India Asteroid Search Campaign, International Astronomical Search Collaboration
Tracked near-Earth objects in real-time sky data with Astrometrica.
